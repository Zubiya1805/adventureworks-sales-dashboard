# AdventureWorks Sales Performance Dashboard

A 3-page Power BI report analysing reseller sales for the AdventureWorks sample company.

## Data
Microsoft AdventureWorks public sample data: 55,555 order lines, 3,590 orders,
334 products, 631 resellers, 17 salespeople, 6 countries (Jul 2017 - May 2020).
2017 and 2020 are partial years.

## What the dashboard shows
- KPI cards: sales, cost, quantity and orders, plus a sales target gauge
- Sales trends over time with year and category slicers
- Sales by region and country (maps and funnel)
- Reseller business types and salesperson performance

## Key insights
- Total sales were $74.1M and 195K units; 2019 was the best full year at $30.8M.
- North America made 84% of sales (US alone 70%).
- Bikes made 83% of sales, but their cost was higher than their sales,
  so overall margin was only about 1.1%.
- Components were the most profitable category (about +$0.94M).

## Tools
Power BI, DAX, data modelling (Sales, Reseller, Salesperson, Territory tables)

## How to open
Download AdventureWorks.pbix and open it in Power BI Desktop.
